Clips Library

Find what you captured last week without digging through folders.

Open the library

Choose Clips Library… in the macOS menu bar menu, or Clips Library in the Windows tray popup. For your newest captures, Recent Captures in the same menu is faster.

Find a capture

  • Search across your screenshots, videos, and GIFs.
  • Filter by capture type.
  • On Windows, filter by date (Today, This week, This month) with the Sort & filter flyout in the toolbar.

Organize

Star your favorites, and group captures with tags and collections.

Reopen and share

  • Open a capture, or reopen it in the editor or trimmer.
  • Copy it, or reveal it in Finder or File Explorer.
  • Open a Studio recording again with Open in Studio….
  • Delete a capture you don't need. This can't be undone on macOS, which warns you first.

Uploadcare uploads

You can upload clips and get shareable links through your own Uploadcare account. This is off until you set it up. Tiny Clips doesn't include Uploadcare credentials or manage your account.

Your API keys are stored on your device, in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Locker, and are never sent to Refractored LLC. Clips you upload go to your Uploadcare project under Uploadcare's terms.
