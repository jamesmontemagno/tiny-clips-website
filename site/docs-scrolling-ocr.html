Scrolling capture and OCR

Capture more than fits on screen, and get text out of anything you can see.

Scrolling capture

Stitch a long page or chat into one tall image.

  1. Start a screenshot, then press P or choose Scroll in the capture picker.
  2. Select the region to capture.
  3. Scroll the page as you normally would. The floating panel shows that it's capturing.
  4. Press Done or Enter to stitch the frames. To throw the capture away, press Cancel or Esc.

The finished image follows your usual screenshot settings for saving, the editor, and the clipboard.

When a capture gets very long

Tiny Clips stops a scrolling capture automatically if it gets too large, then saves what it has so far instead of discarding it. Check the end of a very long capture to make sure it includes everything you need.

Copy text from the screen

Select any part of your screen and Tiny Clips copies the text it recognizes to your clipboard.

  • macOS. Choose Copy Text from Region… in the menu bar menu.
  • Windows. Choose Capture Text in the tray popup, or press T in the capture picker.

To start it from a shortcut, assign one in Settings. See Keyboard shortcuts.
