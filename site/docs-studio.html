Studio (Preview)

Record now and choose the look afterward. Studio keeps your screen and camera as separate layers you can keep editing.

Studio is a preview. It's on by default unless you turned it off, and your choice is kept. Change it with Tiny Clips Studio (Preview) in Settings → Studio. Your projects are kept either way.

Start a Studio recording

  • macOS. Choose Studio Recording… in the menu bar menu.
  • Windows. Choose the Studio tile in the tray popup.

When you stop recording, the Studio editor opens. Record Video and its shortcut always make a standard recording.

Set the look

  • Pick a background, with solid colors or gradients, then adjust the padding and round the corners of the screen.
  • Choose one of four layouts: screen only, camera bubble, side by side, or camera only.
  • Choose a camera shape, and drag the camera bubble where you want it.
  • Crop the edges of the screen or camera from each one's panel.

Zooms, scenes, cuts, and speed

Press a key to add an edit at the playhead. Then drag it on the timeline, or set its details in the panel.

Keys that add edits to the Studio timeline
Key Edit What it does
ZZoomZooms in on a fixed point or follows the pointer. Suggest Zooms proposes zooms from your clicks.
SSplitStarts a new scene with its own layout, which can change instantly or animate into place.
XCutTakes a second out of the video. Drag its ends to change how much.
RSpeedPlays two seconds faster. Rates run from 0.25× to 8×, and changed stretches play without sound.

Press Delete to remove the selected zoom, cut, or speed change. Shift+Left and Shift+Right move the playhead a second, and Home and End go to the start and end.

Annotations

Add these from the Annotate panel or with a key:

  • A arrow, C callout, and T text
  • H rectangle and E ellipse
  • B redaction, which hides an area with blur, pixelation, or a solid color
  • M emoji

Drag an annotation in the preview to move it, and use its handles to resize or rotate it. Attach it to the recording to keep it on what it covers through zooms, or to the frame to keep it in place. To duplicate one, press Ctrl+D on Windows or ⌘+D on macOS.

Captions

Use the Captions panel to add and fix captions:

  • Generate. Make captions from the recording's sound, on your computer. On a Mac, this needs macOS 26 or later and hardware that supports it. On Windows, you choose a speech model, which downloads the first time you use it.
  • Import and export. Use SRT or WebVTT files.
  • Edit and fix. Correct, split, merge, or delete captions. Auto Fix repairs captions that are too short or too fast to read where it can.
  • Draw on the video. Off by default. Choose from four looks and three sizes, at the top or bottom. Viewers can't turn these off, so export a caption file as well if that matters.

Export a video

Choose Export to save an MP4. Pick a resolution (Source, 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p, or 480p) and a quality (Best, High, Standard, or Small File). You'll see the pixel size and estimated file size first. It starts at 1080p and High, then offers your last choice. A video keeps its canvas's shape and is never scaled up.

Save and reopen projects

  • Save Project… saves a copy as a folder you choose, with the recordings and a .tinyclips file that opens them in Studio on this computer or another.
  • Reopen a project from Open in Studio… in the Clips Library, from Recent Captures, or from the drafts list in Settings → Studio.
  • When you close a recording you haven't saved as a project, Studio asks whether to keep it as a draft or delete it.
  • Settings → Studio also sets how long projects are kept and how much disk space they can use.
