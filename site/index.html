One small app. The whole capture loop.

Tiny Clips stays out of your way until you need it, then gives screenshots, recordings, and GIFs exactly the tools they need before they leave your desktop.

01

Capture

Pick a target in one keystroke and Tiny Clips takes care of the rest.

  • Region, screen, or window picker — press R, S, or W
  • Scrolling capture stitches a long page into one tall image
  • Copy text from screen with built-in OCR
  • Optional countdown with a bright region outline
  • Direct hotkeys that skip the picker, all rebindable
  • Multi-monitor aware with per-display targeting
02

Record

Hardware-accelerated MP4 and GIF recording with the audio and overlays creators actually use.

  • H.264 MP4 at 24, 30, or 60 fps; GIFs with custom fps and width
  • Microphone + system audio with device pickers and a soft-knee limiter
  • Webcam overlay — circle, rounded, or square; any corner, any size
  • Teleprompter that scrolls your script and never shows up in the recording
  • Mouse-click highlights, pause/resume, per-recording mute and time limits
  • Optional "Captured on Tiny Clips" watermark and keep-display-awake
03

Polish

A real editor and real trimmers open the instant a capture finishes — no extra app required.

  • Annotate with rectangles, ellipses, straight or curved arrows, lines, freehand, rich text, and numbered badges
  • Redact with blur, pixelate, or solid blocks
  • Backgrounds — gradients, padding, rounded corners, and shadows
  • Export frames: Original, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:4, 9:16 with 3×3 alignment
  • Crop, resize handles, undo, and 25–400% zoom with pan
  • Trim video and GIF with range handles, speed presets, frame stepping, frame export, and remove-audio
04

Organize & share

Find what you captured last week without digging through folders.

  • Clips Library on Windows: browse, open, copy, reveal, and share every capture
  • Clips Manager on Mac: search, tags, notes, collections, favorites, and batch actions
  • Reopen the 10 most recent clips straight from the tray or menu bar
  • Auto-copy to clipboard and reveal in Explorer or Finder
  • Optional Uploadcare uploads with shareable links, using your own account
  • Local capture analytics: daily counts, busiest day, most active hour

Tiny Clips Studio (Preview)

Record now. Choose the look afterward. Studio is an opt-in preview for macOS and Windows that keeps your screen and optional camera as separate editable layers, rather than baking them into one video.

  • Arrange your layers — change the layout and the camera's position, size, and shape after recording
  • Make it yours with backgrounds, padding, and rounded corners
  • Trim and export an MP4, with a project you can reopen to edit again

Studio is off by default, and ordinary video recording stays unchanged when it is off. How to try the preview.

Questions, answered

Anything else? Open an issue on GitHub or use File a Bug inside the app.

How do I install Tiny Clips on Windows?

Open the Microsoft Store listing and click Install. The Store keeps Tiny Clips updated for you. Prefer a terminal? Run winget install Refractored.TinyClips. Signed MSIX packages are also on GitHub Releases.

How do I install Tiny Clips on macOS?

Use the Mac App Store for automatic updates and Clips Manager. Homebrew users can tap jamesmontemagno/tiny-clips and brew install --cask tiny-clips. TestFlight and direct GitHub downloads are available too.

Is Tiny Clips really free?

Yes. Every feature is included on both platforms with no account, subscription, or paid unlock. On macOS you can optionally leave a tip; supporters get a small badge in Settings and nothing else changes.

Does Tiny Clips collect any data?

No. There is no telemetry or analytics service. Capture statistics in Settings → Analytics are stored only on your device. Uploads happen only if you connect your own Uploadcare account. Read the privacy policy.

What permissions does it need?

Windows and macOS prompt for screen capture the first time you record. Microphone and camera are requested only when you turn those options on for a recording, and you can revoke them any time in system settings.

Which formats and quality settings are available?

Screenshots save as PNG (JPEG and scale options on Windows). Video is hardware-accelerated H.264 MP4 at 24, 30, or 60 fps with optional microphone and system audio. GIFs support 5–30 fps and a configurable maximum width.

How do I try Tiny Clips Studio?

Studio is a work-in-progress preview for macOS and Windows, not part of the current store releases. Build the Studio preview branch and check the preview status for current limitations.

In that build, open Settings → Studio and turn on Tiny Clips Studio (Preview). On macOS, choose Studio Recording… from the menu bar menu. On Windows, choose the Studio tile in the tray menu. Record, then arrange your layers in Studio and export an MP4.

Studio is off by default. With Studio off, ordinary video recording stays unchanged; the existing Video command still uses the regular recording workflow.

Which versions of Windows and macOS are supported?

Windows 11 22H2 or later on x64 and ARM64, and macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Both builds are native: WinUI 3 on Windows and Swift with ScreenCaptureKit on Mac.
