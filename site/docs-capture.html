Capture basics

Tiny Clips lives in your menu bar or system tray, one click or shortcut away.

Start a capture

  1. Click the Tiny Clips icon in the macOS menu bar or Windows system tray.
  2. Choose a screenshot, a video, or a GIF.
  3. Choose a region, screen, or window in the capture picker. You can also set an optional countdown.
  4. For recordings, confirm your audio and other options, then record. Stop from the floating controls or with the stop shortcut.

On Windows, the tray popup shows a tile for each capture type with its current shortcut, plus Capture Text. On macOS, the same actions are in the menu bar menu.

Choose what to capture

Keys you can press in the capture picker
Key What it does
RRegion: draw a rectangle around any area
SScreen: capture an entire display
WWindow: capture a specific app window
PScroll, for screenshots only: see Scrolling capture
EscCancel

When you pick a window, the window under the pointer is highlighted with its app, title, and size. Click to capture it, or press Tab and Shift+Tab to move between windows and Enter to select. Esc or a right-click cancels.

Where captures go

  • Save folder. Captures save to folders you choose in Settings, and each capture type can have its own folder.
  • After a capture. Tiny Clips can open the editor or trimmer, or save straight away. Settings also lets you copy each capture to the clipboard and reveal it in Finder or File Explorer.
  • Formats. Video saves as MP4. Choose the screenshot format in Settings: PNG or JPEG, plus WebP on macOS.
  • Finding it again. Reopen a recent capture from Recent Captures in the menu bar menu or tray popup, or open the Clips Library.
