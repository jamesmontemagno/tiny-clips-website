Video and GIF recording

Record your screen to MP4 or an animated GIF, with the extras you need for a clear demo.

Make a recording

  1. Choose video or GIF from the menu bar menu or tray popup, or press its shortcut.
  2. Choose a region, screen, or window. Add a countdown if you want one.
  3. Confirm your options, then start recording.
  4. Stop from the floating controls or with the stop shortcut: CtrlShiftS on Windows or ⌘. on macOS.

You can pause and resume while recording, and set a per-recording mute or time limit. Record Video always makes a standard recording. To keep the screen and camera as separate layers, use Studio.

Video and GIF settings

  • Video. Hardware-accelerated H.264 MP4 at 24, 30, or 60 fps.
  • GIF. A configurable frame rate from 5 to 30 fps and a configurable maximum width.
  • Extras. Mouse-click highlights, an optional "Captured on Tiny Clips" watermark, and an option to keep the display awake.

Audio

Record system audio, your microphone, or both. Choose your microphone in Settings → Video. Tiny Clips asks for microphone access only when you turn that option on.

Webcam overlay

Add a live webcam feed to a video recording. In Settings → Video, choose a circle, rounded, or square shape, a corner, and a size.

Teleprompter

Read from a script while you record. The script scrolls in a small panel that never appears in the recording, and you can drag the panel where you want it.

  1. Open Settings → Teleprompter.
  2. Paste your script or load it from a text file.
  3. Turn on the teleprompter, set the scroll speed, and preview it.
  4. Start a video recording. The panel appears and scrolls as you record.

On Windows, the panel doesn't appear at all if Windows can't keep it out of the capture.

Trim the result

The trimmer can open after a recording. Turn this on or off with Open trimmer after recording in Settings. It works for both video and GIFs:

  • Drag the range handles to keep only the part you want.
  • Step frame by frame, and choose a speed preset.
  • Export a single frame as an image.
  • Remove the audio from a video.
