Permissions and troubleshooting

What Tiny Clips needs to run, and how to fix the most common problems.

System requirements

  • Windows. Windows 11 22H2 or later, on x64 or ARM64.
  • macOS. macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording on macOS. Tiny Clips needs this permission to capture anything. macOS asks on first launch. After you grant it, restart Tiny Clips.
  • Microphone and camera. Tiny Clips asks only when you turn on those options for a recording. Your audio and webcam footage go into that local recording and nowhere else.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in macOS System Settings or Windows Settings.

Keep Tiny Clips updated

  • Microsoft Store and Mac App Store. The store keeps the app updated.
  • winget. Run winget upgrade Refractored.TinyClips.
  • Direct Mac downloads. Choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar menu.

Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to fix them
Problem What to do
A Mac capture doesn't work after you changed permissionsCheck System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then restart Tiny Clips.
A recording has no microphone or cameraTurn the option on for the recording, and check that your OS still allows microphone or camera access.
A shortcut doesn't workCheck or change it in Settings → Shortcuts on macOS or Settings → Keyboard shortcuts on Windows. Some shortcuts have no default until you set them.
You can't find a captureCheck Recent Captures or the Clips Library, or open your save folder from the menu bar menu or tray popup.
The editor or trimmer doesn't openCheck that it's turned on in Settings. With it off, captures save straight away.
Studio isn't in the menuTurn on Tiny Clips Studio (Preview) in Settings → Studio.
An agent can't connectSee Agent Control troubleshooting.
